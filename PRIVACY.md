# AmpWright privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

AmpWright is an Android app from Offshoot Works for monitoring batteries over Bluetooth. It does not
collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data.

## What the app does not do

- It has no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking.
- It does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the internet itself.

## What stays on your phone

- **The last battery you connected to.** Its Bluetooth name and address are saved so the app can
  reconnect when it opens. Tap "Forget" on the connect screen to remove them.
- **Details of the last crash, if the app crashes.** These are saved so they can be included in a
  diagnostics report. They contain technical details about the app, not personal information.
- **Readings from your battery** are shown on screen while you are connected and are not saved.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this.

## Permissions

- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth)** is used only to find and connect to your battery. On Android 12 and
  later, the app tells Android it does not use Bluetooth to work out your location.
- **Location** is only requested on Android 11 and older, where Android requires it for any Bluetooth
  scanning. The app never reads your location.

## Diagnostics reports

The About & diagnostics screen can build a report to help with bug reports. It contains the app and
Android versions, your phone model, connection events and the data your battery sent. Most of the
battery's Bluetooth address is hidden.

The report is only shared if you choose to, using Share or Copy. If you paste it into a GitHub issue,
it becomes public on GitHub and is covered by
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
